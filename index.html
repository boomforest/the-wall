How notes turn red
  • High stakes: red 14 days before the date, orange from 30.
  • Medium: red 7 days before, orange from 21.
  • Low: red 3 days before, orange from 10.
  • Trigger date: red on that day no matter what. Use it for "if I haven't heard back by…".
  • Anything of yours past its date is red, and a red note shows what happens if it slips.
  • Waiting on someone or parked: stays blue until its trigger date, even if the due date passes.
Your notes stay on this device

Nothing is sent anywhere. Notes are saved in this browser only, so back them up now and then, or to move to another device.
