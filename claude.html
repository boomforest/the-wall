Here's how I'd pin that

On fire

Overdue, close to the date for how much is at stake, or a trigger date has passed.

Coming up

On the radar. These turn red by themselves when it's time.

On the wall

Real, no deadline pressing yet.

Off your brain

Waiting on someone else, or parked on purpose. If a trigger date passes, the note comes back red.

Done
How notes turn red
  • High stakes: red 14 days before the date, orange from 30.
  • Medium: red 7 days before, orange from 21.
  • Low: red 3 days before, orange from 10.
  • Trigger date: red on that day no matter what. Use it for "if I haven't heard back by…".
  • Anything of yours past its date is red, and a red note shows what happens if it slips.
  • Waiting on someone or parked: stays blue until its trigger date, even if the due date passes.

Note

Rearrange card changes dates, triggers and who's up, right here. Hand to Virgil is for real work (emails, research, drafts, forms): Virgil does it and writes back on the card. Closing the card keeps what you wrote.
